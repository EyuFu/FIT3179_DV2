The Open-Air Gym: How Australians Exercise Outdoors

Where, what, which state and why: outdoor exercise across Australian ages, activities and places.

Parks, footpaths, trails and beaches are Australia’s most popular places to exercise. In 2025 they were used by about twice as many adults as gyms and leisure centres.

Drawing on AusPlay, the Australian Bureau of Statistics, the Bureau of Meteorology and OpenStreetMap, this page follows four questions: where, what, which state, and why.

1. Places — Where Do Australians Work Out?

AusPlay, the national survey of sport and physical activity, asks adults where they were active over the past year. In 2025, 62 in every 100 adults used a public space — a park, street, trail or beach. Only 31 used a gym or leisure centre, and 30 exercised at home.

Age changes the picture. Outdoor exercise rises through middle age to a peak of 72% among 55 to 64 year-olds, while gym use falls from 45% to 16% between 18 to 24 year-olds and people aged 65 and over. People can use several places, so each row in chart 1 is counted separately.

2. Activities — What Do We Do, and What Is Changing?

Walking for exercise has been the most popular activity in every survey year since 2016, and bushwalking ranks third in 2024 and 2025. Age matters too: walking keeps growing into later life.

In 2025 the biggest change was indoors: gym and fitness rose by almost 4 percentage points, while outdoor activities held steady. Most other activities peak in the teenage years.

3. States — Where in Australia?

Every state and territory is mostly outdoors, but the share differs. Chart 6 shows who exercises outdoors in each state, and chart 7 shows what your own state does differently.

Charts 8 and 9 turn to the equipment: where the free outdoor fitness stations are, and how many there are for each person. Three different maps answer three different questions.

4. Motives — Why, How Often and When?

Health and fitness is the most common reason to be active (67% of active adults), but 51% also exercise to be outdoors or enjoy nature. When to head outside depends on your city’s weather (chart 12).

For people who are not active, the most common barriers are poor health or injury (27%) and feeling too old (23%). Disability (15%) and not having enough time (13%) also hold people back.

5. Conclusion

Outdoor spaces are where Australians exercise most: 62% used a public space in 2025, against 31% at a gym. Walking is the top activity every year and keeps growing into later life.

However, the National Health Survey 2022 found that just 24% of people aged 15 and over met the national guidelines. Free outdoor stations (chart 8) and the mildest months (chart 12) make starting easier.

Notes on the data

Activity names have been written in plain English (for example, AusPlay’s “Walking (Recreational)” is shown as “Walking for exercise”). Shares are survey estimates and are rounded.

AusPlay moved from phone interviews to an online survey in July 2023, so results from 2016–2022 and from 2024 onwards are not directly comparable. Chart 3 is the only chart that crosses this change, and it keeps the two periods separate.

OpenStreetMap is mapped by volunteers, so charts 8 and 9 show mapped places only.
